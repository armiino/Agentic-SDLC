# Requirements

## Functional Requirements:
1. Customer Portal: 
   - Users can login via email and possibly Single Sign-On (SSO).
   - The portal supports viewing orders and downloading invoices.
2. Sales needs to generate offers quickly.

## Non-functional Requirements:
- Performance should be "fast".
- No new database server, managed services are preferred.
- Support legacy ERP integration (SAP).

## Constraints/Compliance:
1. Compliance issues with DSGVO related to email login and data handling.
2. Auditability and logging requirements need clarification.
